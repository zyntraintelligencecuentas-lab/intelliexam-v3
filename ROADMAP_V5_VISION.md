# 🚀 INTELLIEXAM V5: THE "SCALING" PHASE
**Vision**: Become the standard AI Operating System for teachers in Latin America.  
**Strategy**: Transition from a utility tool to a critical infrastructure for schools.

---

## 🏗️ PHASE 1: TECHNOLOGICAL REBIRTH (Month 1)
- **Next.js 15 Migration**: Move the Vanilla SPA to Next.js for SSR (Server Side Rendering) and better SEO.
- **Microservices Refactor**: Separate the AI engine from the core API to allow independent scaling.
- **Turso Edge Expansion**: Optimize data replication for multi-region access.

## 💳 PHASE 2: REVENUE & GROWTH (Month 2)
- **Stripe Integration**:
  - *Tier 1 (Free)*: 3 Scans/month, Basic AI chat.
  - *Tier 2 (Pro)*: $149 MXN/mo. Unlimited Scans, Full NEM 2022 Planning, Export to PDF/Word.
  - *Tier 3 (School)*: Multi-teacher dashboard, institutional analytics.
- **Marketing Engine**: referral program for teachers ("Invite a colleague and get 1 month Pro free").

## 🎙️ PHASE 3: THE "WOW" FEATURES (Month 3)
- **VoiceCore**: Integration with Whisper API to allow dictation of student observations and lesson plans.
- **RAG Library**: A searchable database of all SEP (Mexico) official curriculum documents, linked directly to the chat.
- **Offline Sync**: Ability to grade and take notes without internet, syncing back to Turso when connected.

---

## 📈 BUSINESS KPIs (V5 Goals)
1. **Retention**: 40% of monthly active users.
2. **Conversion**: 5% of free users moving to Pro.
3. **Efficiency**: Reduce manual planning time for teachers by 80%.

---
**CEO/Senior Dev Note**: V5 is not about adding "more buttons", it's about stability, monetization, and being the most reliable partner for the teacher.
